cell therefore requires a label and solid-angle measure; applying another

For dimensionless capacity \(\widehat C\), proper delay
\(\widehat\delta\), global metric length \(L_g\), and cell measure
\(\Delta\widehat x\,\Delta\Omega\),

\[
C_{\rm J}=\frac{c^4}{G}L_g\widehat C\,
\Delta\widehat x\,\Delta\Omega,
\qquad
\delta_{\rm s}=\frac{L_g}{c}\widehat\delta.
\]

Thus the power scale and node action satisfy

\[
\frac{C_{\rm J}}{\delta_{\rm s}}
=\frac{c^5}{G}\frac{\widehat C}{\widehat\delta}
\Delta\widehat x\,\Delta\Omega,
\qquad
\frac{C_{\rm J}\delta_{\rm s}}{\hbar}
=\left(\frac{L_g}{\ell_P}\right)^2
\widehat C\widehat\delta\,
\Delta\widehat x\,\Delta\Omega.
\]

fixed cell measure. Carrier mass per charge, electrical inventory, vortex
