# Source construction restart: deferred options and retained constraints

Date: 9 September 2026.

The [material-confined fermion evaluation](CONFINED_FERMION_EVALUATION.md)
now supplies a bounded test of the first deferred direction. A counted scalar
mass well produces 74, 302 and 1,222 resolved positive-frequency multiplets
at three couplings; every occupied multiplet opposes the required integrated
opening. This ends the particle-occupation repair in that model. Its absolute
vacuum stress remains a separate source question. Finite-core vortex strings
provide another material setting whose spectrum, forces and useful signed
stress still require a specified construction.

The [archived geometry comparison](ARCHIVED_GEOMETRY_SOURCE_REASSESSMENT.md)
leaves the small-coefficient ideal longitudinal source excluded. Its remaining
components already have arbitrary aggregate stress subject to nonnegative
radial null projection. Reassigning ordinary material responsibilities therefore
preserves that exclusion. A useful source revision changes the supplied negative
radial-null stress, its spectrum or global path, or the geometric assumptions.

## Fermion literature qualification

[Weinbaum's 2026 analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28738v1),
[accepted by Physical Review D on 31 August](https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/g89c-gqyf),
challenges the frequency and electromagnetic self-interaction treatment in
earlier EDM constructions. Its self-consistency searches concern neutral,
static, spherical Dirac sources in specified frequency, angular-momentum and
parity classes; they encounter obstructions to a complete wormhole. Section
IV.C nevertheless exhibits a normalized positive-frequency stationary state
with negative averaged null stress on a prescribed geometry. This supports a
component-level question while weakening the earlier complete-source examples.

Equation (111) also requires an attractive lapse well for freely bound massive
states. Reading the existing
[reference cache](data/archived_geometry_opening/reference_metric.npz)
gives 16,385 samples on
`[-8,8]`, minimum lapse 1, maximum 108.071032785761, and lapse 1 at both
endpoints. Material confinement therefore deserves explicit treatment in any
fermion revisit. The cache observation supplies a screening condition, with
the complete exterior and quantum state belonging to the proposed model.

The hybrid proposal is an inference: let the backbone and finite transition
carry their assigned material duties, while a separately normalized fermion
state supplies a candidate signed stress. External electromagnetic or Yukawa
confinement must arise from counted material fields. Its availability and
radial-null cost remain construction questions. The earlier wall gas and the
full Dirac stress are different approximations to different proposed roles.

## Why the other comparisons retain lower priority

[String-based wormhole constructions](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.07821v2)
use compact string modes for Casimir stress alongside separate mechanical
support. They motivate the component arrangement. The unresolved step here is
deriving a source beyond the excluded ideal channel approximation at the
rail's scale, with the core, returns and supporting material included.

[Maldacena and Milekhin](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.06618v2) obtain large
effective channel counts from a Randall–Sundrum sector. Their construction
introduces physics beyond the Standard Model and a large difference between
interior and exterior elapsed time. It provides a magnitude mechanism, with
a substantial theory and operational matching cost.

The original [conformal scalar wormholes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9908029)
cross the critical effective gravitational coupling. The
[scalar–tensor nonexistence result](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1005.3262),
equations (1)–(5), covers static asymptotically flat wormholes with smooth
positive gravitational and scalar kinetic factors and NEC-respecting additional
matter. Adding ordinary support preserves those assumptions. A quantum hybrid
changes the additional-matter premise but retains a separately required
negative-null source.

[Butcher's cylindrical calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1283) already
identifies its vanishing exactly longitudinal null stress as the obstruction to
static self-support. Negative energy density alone leaves this particular
rail requirement open.

## Bounded decision before another coupled solve

The completed fermion audit specifies the confining interaction, physical
particle states and normalizations, and separates occupied-state stress from
absolute vacuum stress. Its integrated opening gate ends the evaluated
particle-occupation branch after the eigenmode search. Numerical controls
include an independently solved mode and reproduction of a published
negative-ANEC fermion state. Reopening this direction requires a specific
change to the signed source mechanism; a full semiclassical calculation
retains its own absolute-vacuum and material-force requirements.

Meanwhile, [independent-current transport](COMER_TWO_CURRENT_EVOLUTION_ROUND.md),
the regular condensate transition, the standing backbone role, and the angular
and reservoir responsibilities remain part of the
[component architecture](RAIL_COMPONENT_CROSS_REFERENCE_AND_JOINT_COORDINATION.md).
Their established roles make a hybrid more specific. They leave the signed
source mechanism as the deciding uncertainty.
