# Source-construction selection constraints

Decision date: 10 September 2026.

The prestressed backbone and heat-buffer assembly is set aside from the
active search following the
[feasibility investigation](RESERVOIR_FEASIBILITY_ENVELOPE.md).
Its reduced material velocity, field interpretation, and measured source
requirements remain useful comparison results. Further selection prioritizes
assemblies with an independently supplied complete source and physically
specified component responses.

## Default source choices

The source and structure search prioritizes electromagnetic fields, material
stresses, fluids, interacting currents, and quantum fields with explicit
material boundaries and state preparation. Each candidate retains the rail's
separate support, angular, transport, storage, and handoff duties.

String matter and ideal string-cloud sources are deferred by the user's
selection preference. Reconsideration requires a concrete, substantial
improvement in an assembly's potential viability relative to its best
available construction without such sources. The comparison includes the
full tensor, compensating-source requirement, confinement, preparation,
interfaces, and operating cycle. A useful algebraic fit alone establishes
an effective representation and leaves physical selection open.

Ordinary fibers, cables, and concrete vortex or magnetic flux-tube
constructions remain eligible under their specified material and field laws,
with their mass, stresses, and boundary loads included. Their physical
properties determine the available tension and response speed. Selection
compares the required physical capabilities, their evidence, and their
assembly benefit; the word "string" alone supplies no physics ranking.

## Consequence of the latest trial

The tested radial-string contribution has
\(p_r=-\rho\), zero radial current, and hence zero projection onto either
radial null direction. Adding this term at fixed geometry and fixed other
tensors leaves the diagnosed radial-null shortfall unchanged. The restoring
pressure in the local decomposition comes from transverse magnetic
compression. The trial establishes no exceptional viability benefit that
would justify selecting a string source.

The assembly's feasibility audit supplies a reusable bound coupling buffer
inertia, restoring response, material velocity, and the remaining source
allowance. Physical size stays a variable. Future comparisons use supplied
physical capacities and their uncertainty; earlier assembly multipliers
remain comparative quantities.
