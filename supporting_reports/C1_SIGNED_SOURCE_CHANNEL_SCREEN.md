# C1 signed source: finite reflecting channels and tensor completion

17 September 2026.

The multicomponent comparison retains separate radial and angular signed-source
roles, the Maxwell field and ordinary supporting material. With the angular
contribution granted as a target, thirty-two reflecting radial compartments
per module satisfy the sampled bulk inequalities in both retained overlap
brackets. Varying the channel count between compartments substantially reduces
the required inventory and reflector forces. Physical laws for the angular
component, reflectors and their material reactions remain open.

A restricted comparison sets the angular quantum contribution to zero and
asks the radial channels and ordinary material to supply the complete tensor.
All 80 such comparisons fail. Its length-independent energy–pressure identity
quantifies the contribution required from the existing signed angular/transition
role. This restricted exclusion leaves the radial channels eligible as a
component of the conditional multicomponent allocation above.

## Supplied bulk quantum law and finite-end exchange

The quantum model contains independent relativistic conformal channels with
reflecting ends in their strip ground states. A channel has central charge
\(c\geq0\) and optical length \(L=\int dl/A\), where
\(dl=B\,dx\) and \(a=\log A\). The strip Hamiltonian in
[Cardy's boundary-CFT review, equation (27)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0411189)
fixes the ground-state Casimir coefficient. The Weyl transformation in
[Maldacena, Milekhin and Popov, appendix F](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.04726v3#A6)
supplies the curvature terms. The resulting spherical radial average is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_Q&=\frac{\eta c}{4\pi R^2}
\left[-\frac{\pi}{24L^2A^2}
+\frac{2a''+a'^2}{24\pi}\right],\\
p_{r,Q}&=\frac{\eta c}{4\pi R^2}
\left[-\frac{\pi}{24L^2A^2}
-\frac{a'^2}{24\pi}\right],\qquad p_{t,Q}=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Primes denote proper derivatives. The inherited gravitational conversion is
\(\eta=2.4127904527582454\times10^{-5}\). The flat reflecting-strip
coefficient equals the periodic-cylinder coefficient at circumference
\(2L\), accounting for the round trip. Each module's channels are independent
field populations, so their stresses add in their common quantum overlap.

This law specifies an effective longitudinal quantum sector. A physical
guide supplies its relativistic modes, transverse spectrum, reflecting
response and material stress. The source-selection preference continues to
prioritize specified fields and materials; this screen assigns the ordinary
supports their own tensors independently of the channel law.

Within each compartment the bulk conservation equation is

\[
p_{r,Q}'+a'(\rho_Q+p_{r,Q})
+2\frac{R'}R p_{r,Q}=0.
\]

At a reflector, the one-sided pressure jump supplies the finite material
reaction duty

\[
F_{\rm material}=-4\pi R^2[p_{r,Q}]_-^+.
\]

The numerical record retains every jump. Equal optical compartments with
equal central charge balance their internal reflector forces; their outer
end loads grow with the square of the compartment count. Variable channel
counts create additional internal reflector loads. The weak conservation
audit includes all these terms. Reflector mass, boundary polarization,
confinement and preparation remain physical source terms to be supplied.
The listed forces are scalar radial load integrals in the spherical average;
angular-resolved module motion requires the corresponding spatial embedding.

## Restricted radial-only comparison and the angular-source requirement

Subtracting the two radial components eliminates the state-dependent Casimir
term:

\[
\rho_Q-p_{r,Q}=\frac{\eta c}{48\pi^2R^2}(a''+a'^2).
\]

Consequently shortening a cavity increases its negative radial-null stress
while preserving this independent tensor constraint. In the native transition
around \(x=\pm2.5\), the geometric source instead has

\[
(\rho-p_r)_{\rm geometry}\simeq-0.001004672,
\qquad
(\rho-p_r)_{\rm after\ Maxwell}\simeq-0.001037500.
\]

The quantum coefficient multiplying \(\eta c\) there is positive,
approximately \(0.000357601\). Ordinary material satisfying the stated
pressure bound also has \(\rho-p_r\geq0\). Every nonnegative mixture of
these radial conformal bulk sources and ordinary material therefore has the
opposite sign to the required source difference. The geometric difference
itself is already negative, so changing the ordinary Maxwell allocation alone
leaves this witness in place.

The sampled exclusion occupies finite bands from \(-3\) to approximately
\(-2.447\) and from approximately \(2.447\) to \(3\). Finite reflecting
surfaces preserve the obstruction in the intervening bulk. A resolved layer
with additional quantum polarization, a transverse quantum sector or a change
to the geometric source changes the relevant equations. An independent
four-dimensional curvature evaluation confirms the negative geometric
difference at both transition witnesses.

This is an exclusion of radial conformal channels as the entire signed sector
under the ordinary-material bound. Within the multicomponent allocation,
the same identity gives the existing angular target the necessary lower bound

\[
v\geq\max\!\left[0,\frac{(\rho_Q-p_{r,Q})
-(\rho-p_r)_{\rm after\ Maxwell}}4\right].
\]

Its physical realization remains the assigned signed angular/transition task.
The radial-only exclusion supplies this compatibility condition between source
components; the following comparison evaluates their combined allocation.

## Preserving the separate angular-source role

\[

\]

Its radial null projection vanishes. For the remaining tensor
\(t=T_{\rm geometry}-T_E-T_Q\), an ordinary material tensor and this angular
target can complete the pointwise allocation precisely when

\[
t_\rho+t_r\geq0,\qquad t_\rho-t_t\geq0.
\]

The least angular weight at that point is
\(v=\max[0,-(t_\rho-t_r)/4,-(t_\rho+t_t)/4]\).
This retains an explicit additional signed-source requirement. Its physical
state and boundaries remain part of the joint construction.

comparisons plus eight metric-sampling and quadrature controls. Independent
cases use four worker processes. All 80 radial-only comparisons fail; 20
angular-target relaxations satisfy their sampled bulk inequalities, all at

field tensors, reflector forces and complete bulk allocations. The successful
bulk comparisons retain their measured off-grid inequalities and refinement
limits in the [audit](data/c1_signed_channels/verification.json).

check of 92 parent hashes. All 140 failed source comparisons have independently
reconstructed pointwise exclusion witnesses. The largest retained bulk-tensor
reconstruction error is \(3.33\times10^{-15}\); the normalized weak-conservation
error is below \(3.24\times10^{-16}\). The largest inventory change across the
sampling, metric and quadrature controls is 0.00499%. The finest off-grid
stress-bound deficit is at most \(1.991\times10^{-7}\), or
\(2.77\times10^{-6}\) of the peak geometric stress. These sampled bulk
