# White Casimir Physical Readout Sources

This folder contains local PDF sources used by
`../white_casimir_physical_readout_paths_paper.tex`.

## Core comparison source

- `white_et_al_custom_casimir_geometry_epjc_2021.pdf`  
  White, Vera, Han, Bruccoleri, and MacArthur, "Worldline numerics applied to
  custom Casimir geometry generates unanticipated intersection with Alcubierre
  warp metric," EPJ C 81, 677 (2021).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09484-z

## Casimir force and pressure metrology

- `decca_aksyuk_lopez_casimir_mems_nems_2011.pdf`  
  Decca, Aksyuk, and Lopez, "Casimir force in micro and nano electro
  mechanical systems," in *Casimir Physics* (2011).  
  NIST PDF: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=906575

- `bimonte_et_al_casimir_force_0p2_8um_2021.pdf`  
  Bimonte et al., "Measurement of the Casimir Force between 0.2 and 8 um:
  Experimental Procedures and Comparison with Theory," *Universe* 7, 93 (2021).  
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03857

- `stange_et_al_commercial_mems_casimir_metrology_2019.pdf`  
  Stange, Imboden, Javor, Barrett, and Bishop, "Building a Casimir metrology
  platform with a commercial MEMS sensor," *Microsystems & Nanoengineering* 5,
  14 (2019).  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41378-019-0054-5

## Resonator and superconducting machinery

- `garcia_sanchez_et_al_nanomebrane_surface_potential_prl_2012.pdf`  
  Garcia-Sanchez et al., "Casimir Force and In Situ Surface Potential
  Measurements on Nanomembranes," *Physical Review Letters* 109, 027202 (2012).  
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4429

- `garcia_sanchez_et_al_high_q_sin_casimir_probe_2013.pdf`  
  Garcia-Sanchez et al., "Casimir probe based upon metallized high Q SiN
  nanomembrane resonator" (2013).  
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.7025

- `perez_et_al_casimir_energy_condensation_nems_2020.pdf`  
  Perez et al., "A system for probing Casimir energy corrections to the
  condensation energy," *Microsystems & Nanoengineering* 6, 1 (2020).  
  NIST PDF: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=930132

- `de_jong_et_al_superconducting_casimir_force_2025.pdf`  
  de Jong et al., "Measurement of the Casimir force between superconductors"
  (2025).  
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13759

- `gurevich_tuning_microwave_losses_superconducting_resonators_2023.pdf`  
  Gurevich, "Tuning microwave losses in superconducting resonators,"
  *Superconductor Science and Technology* 36, 073002 (2023).  
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07491
